# Deliverable B. Product invariants

These are constraints every future PRD, schema and route must satisfy. Each one is stated as something the system does or refuses to do, with the test that proves it. Provenance tags as in Deliverable A; **[R-prov]** marks a line that appears in an approved document but is itself tagged PROVISIONAL there. Nine invariants; if a tenth is proposed it should replace one of these, not join them.

| # | Invariant | Concrete constraint | Proof (a test or a structural property) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | **Presented terms are immutable.** | A version of a shoot's terms, once presented to any participant, is never modified. Any edit after presentation produces a new version. Acceptances reference a version id, never the working row. | Evidence tables have no UPDATE or DELETE grant for the application role; a database trigger rejects both; a test attempts each through the API and through SQL. Evidence blobs (record PDFs) live in a write-once storage class the runtime credentials cannot delete or overwrite; a test proves it. | [R] DOCTRINE 8-element set; SP-007; SP-001 |
| I2 | **Evidence outlives everything around it.** | Deleting a shoot, a participant, a profile or an account archives operational state and never removes agreement evidence. Foreign keys from evidence to identity and collaboration parents are RESTRICT, not CASCADE. Purge is a separate, audited, dry-run-first command whose policy Cory and counsel set; until then no purge path exists. | Migration lint fails on any CASCADE toward an evidence table; account-closure test asserts the other party can still retrieve the record. | [R] DOCTRINE "archive, not destroy"; retention period [OPEN], D9 |
| I3 | **Consequential policy is enforced by the server, deny-by-default.** | Every command declares the actor scope it requires; a command with no declared policy is unreachable. Ownership is checked on the object being mutated, not a parent. Safety rules (for example the future minor/nudity gate) exist first as server functions that reject a command. | A test iterates the command registry and fails CI if any command lacks a policy declaration or a negative test (unauthenticated, wrong actor, suspended, expired bearer). | [R-prov] DOCTRINE:79 "server-side and tested against negative cases" (tagged PROVISIONAL there); charge §18; SP-002/003/004/027 |
| I4 | **Reviewing, agreeing and holding a record are never behind an entitlement.** | For every participant, including those who create an account only to respond: reading a presented version, raising a concern, agreeing, receiving the record, and generating every document the record triggers are free. Documents and PDFs are free for everyone, including when the other party never uses the product. Whether *initiating* a shoot is free or part of a paid plan is Cory's (D7); the boundary is drawn at the invitee side, which no plan may touch. | The Agreement & Evidence, Documents and Communications domains cannot import Entitlements (lint, added when Entitlements exists, stage 6); a test runs an invitee with no plan through review, agreement, record and documents. | [R] VISION:469 (documents, releases, PDFs free, no exceptions); [CA] COST-REALITY:100 (invitee participation free); the general "safety never paywalled" is [R-prov] DOCTRINE:67 |
| I5 | **Compensation is explicit or the terms are not presentable.** | A version cannot be presented while any participant's compensation is unspecified, "TBD" or "negotiable". Paid terms carry an amount as a number with a currency, a deposit if any, and payment timing. Trade terms name what each party gives and receives. Which *other* blocks are mandatory for presentation is Cory's (D4). | Presentation command rejects incomplete compensation; the vocabulary has no negotiable value. | [R] DOCTRINE:65 (compensation explicit; no negotiable); [R] DOCTRINE:48 (amount, down payment, timing); the extension to other blocks is [F], D4 |
| I6 | **Nothing is browsable by default.** | A signed-in account is shown nothing about any other person until that person deliberately shares something with them, or posts work that invites reply. Every grant of access is scoped to one thing, has an expiry, and is revocable by its owner. No endpoint lists people. | The command registry has no people-listing query; grant tests assert scope, expiry and revocation. | [R] VISION Tensions 3 and 4; [CA] "no pull without a prior push" |
| I7 | **The product claims only what it enforces.** | No simulated capability exists in the production build: a feature is real and tested, or it is absent. Copy about signing, identity, payments, moderation and safety is reviewed against what the server enforces before it ships. The platform records compensation; it does not move money between users. | Copy review is a PR checklist item for any screen naming those topics; no payment rails between accounts exist; no feature flag can enable an unimplemented capability. | [R] DOCTRINE truthfulness; VISION "no payments between users" |
| I8 | **Identity is owned by the product.** | Every account has an opaque, immutable internal id. External logins are linked identities. Sessions carry the internal id only. Account, professional profile, represented talent and organization are distinct concepts even where only the first two are built. An invitation can be claimed only by an account whose verified email matches the invited address. | Schema: no external subject as a primary key; identity-link table; a test links a second login method to an existing account; a negative test refuses a claim from a non-matching account. | [R] SP-008 disposition; charge §19 |
| I9 | **Adults hold accounts; youth is a sealed partition.** | Every account holder is an adult. A minor is never an account. Until verification exists, an account records an adult attestation at first participation and every affirmation carries it (D11); from the first public upload onward, verification is real. The youth ecosystem, when built, is a separate partition with its own content pool, discovery and entry rules; no adult content can be shown inside it. Until Cory and counsel approve youth operation, the partition is present in the model (an audience classification on content and shoots, a represented-profile concept) and disabled. | An account without an adult attestation (v1) or adult verification (stage 3 onward) cannot present, agree or upload; content and shoots carry an audience field with a single accepted value in v1; the youth value is rejected by the server while disabled. | [R] VISION Tension 2, DOCTRINE youth section; [CA] COST-REALITY Rule 3, CORY-QUESTIONS:184,195 (verify at first act of participation; before any public upload) |

## Notes on the set

- I1 and I2 together are what "the product freezes the agreement" means in a database and a bucket.
- I3 is the direct inverse of the old app's central failure (recon C: "no single choke point"). Its doctrine line is provisional; the charge (§18) and the engineering evidence make it a constraint regardless.
- I4 is drawn deliberately at the invitee side. The charge (§10) says creating and setting terms should not be premium; Cory's July ruling **[CA]** makes initiating a brief part of paid presence. That conflict is put to Cory as D7 rather than resolved here.
- I6 is the invariant most at risk from feature pressure later (a "find a photographer near me" request will arrive). The way to add discovery without breaking it is work-first posting (Deliverable G, stage 4), never a directory.
- I9 is the one invariant where the model carries structure for a feature that is not built. That is the cost of "future-compatible, presently disabled"; it is one column and one enum value, not a subsystem. The attestation is the honest v1 answer to "adults only" until a verification vendor is chosen.
