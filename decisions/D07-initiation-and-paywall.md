# D07 — Initiation and the paywall

Status: DECIDED

## Why this needs Cory

What is free, what is paid, and exactly where the line falls. Not prices.

This decision was open in the planning source window and is now directly decided by Cory's 2026-10-05 ruling below.

## Already settled

- All consent documentation, model releases and their PDFs are free, with no exceptions, including when working with people who are not on the app **[R]** (VISION:469).
- Monetization is in volume and professional convenience, never in safety **[R]** (VISION:484).
- Earlier July candidate directions placed the paid line at initiation and described a documents-only free tier **[CA]** (COST-REALITY:84,100). Those positions are historical evidence and are superseded by Cory's directly confirmed 2026-10-05 ruling.

Direction only, not settled:

- "The professional dashboard is definitively not free tier" (VISION:493). It sits in a monetization answer the vision marks "PARTIALLY ANSWERED" (VISION:484), and the vision keeps tiers and entitlements open (VISION:20, VISION:530). It is direction for this decision, not a ratified tier rule.
- A free-tier sketch lists "responding to a limited number of moodboards and briefs" (VISION:479), under a heading marked "PROPOSED... not final" (VISION:475). The later July ruling says invitee participation is free **[CA]**.
- Pricing and tiers are marked DEFERRED in doctrine (DOCTRINE:68).

## Cory's answer

Direct ruling — 2026-10-05.

**The free tier completes the full loop: project/brief, agreement and record.**

The monetization axis is **capacity, never completion**.

Cory's current capacity example is approximately two active collaborations at a time, but the exact number is tunable and is not doctrine.

A user who is permitted to start a collaboration must not hit a paywall that prevents completing its safety, consent, agreement or record loop.

The exact shape of any standalone document tool remains a document-product question under D08; it is no longer a paywall blocker.

Source: `2026-10-05-cory-reconciliation.md`.

### Direct rulings — 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-14/15

Source: [`2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md`](2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md) (Q1, B5). These add to the ruling above; they do not change it.

**Profile and portfolio sit behind the paywall; doing collaborations does not.** (Q1) Participating in collaborations remains free, and the full-loop-free ruling for completing a collaboration is unchanged. No capacity numbers or prices are chosen here.

**Start routes: three, plus shareable links. No fourth route.** (B5) This closes the 2026-10-05 open seam on a fourth route.

- The three known starts stand: talent applies to posted work; someone sends a project directly to a known person; talent converts an off-platform approach into a brief.
- **A referral — including an agent passing a project to their roster — is just a shared link.** Whoever receives it applies through the ordinary route. No special machinery; the creator still never browses anyone; links are a growth mechanism in their own right.
- **A referrer introduces and nothing more.** A referrer never acts for talent, ticks a term, signs, accepts a fee or agrees to a boundary (D12). Link-as-invitation is recorded in D03.
- How each route reaches agreement differs: posted work is wholesale acceptance, direct send is per-term (D02).

## What this unlocks

- Product access rules.
- Any commercial claim.
- Whether documents can exist outside a collaboration.

Billing is not built in the first stages either way. Its absence is not evidence that initiation is free.
