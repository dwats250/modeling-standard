# D12 — Who may act, and for whom

Status: DECIDED

## Why this needs Cory

Whether everyone in the first slice acts only for themselves.

- In the D01 scenario, does every person sign only for themselves?
- If a client, brand, agency or company is involved, who signs for it, and what establishes that they may?
- May anyone commit another person?

Authority is never inferred from controlling an account or an inbox.

The earlier **[F] lean** of "adults acting for themselves only for the first slice" is superseded by Cory's standing no-proxy ruling below.

## Already settled

Nothing for the adult side.

- Guardian-managed profiles for minors are ratified **[R]** (DOCTRINE:86).
- Youth launch is deferred pending professional legal review **[R]** (VISION:463; DOCTRINE:89). That settles the deferral, not the youth design.
- Agents and agencies appear in July tier thinking **[CA]** (CORY-QUESTIONS:163).

## Cory's answer

Direct ruling — 2026-10-05.

No proxies. Nobody signs on another person's behalf. The person acting is the person reading and affirming.

Clients and other non-present parties sign their own applicable documentation. Being the creator, organizer, client, payer or account holder never authorizes someone to affirm for another person.

For the current adult collaboration path, minors are out of scope.

Cory also described witness signatures as potentially useful and optional. Witnessing is a separate evidence feature; it does not create proxy authority and is not required by this decision. *(Superseded 2026-10-15: the "witness" is the emergency contact; see below.)*

The long-term status of the previously ratified deferred youth partition is not changed here. Cory's current statement governs the present build; any permanent removal of the future youth path should be reconciled explicitly against I9 before changing that canon. *(Answered 2026-10-15: not removed, not reopened; see below.)*

### Direct rulings — 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-14/15

Source: [`2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md`](2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md) (B4, B5, C4, Q8, §3.7, §3.8). No-proxy stands unchanged. Scope: photo shoots.

**Referrers introduce and nothing more.** (B5) A referral, including an agent passing a project to their roster, is a shared link (D07). **No agent or referrer ever ticks a term, signs, accepts a fee or agrees to a boundary for talent.** This is where someone will try to bend this decision, so it is stated explicitly.

**Adult product; youth stays deferred.** (C4) It is adult. The question whether that is permanent was rejected as posed ("can you confidently say anything is a permanent shape of any product before launch?"). The deferred youth path is **neither removed nor reopened**; the I9 canon is unchanged. **Zero UI hints:** no placeholders, no "coming soon," nothing greyed out. To a user, a youth path does not exist. No minor release exists (D08).

**Presence.** (§3.8, Q8)

- **Nobody is in the room who is not in the agreement. Period.** "If you want to get your makeup done before you go to the shoot, good for you. But they cannot be in the room if they're not in the agreement."
- **A child cannot be present, because a child cannot currently be a party.** Children are excluded by this rule's construction; the product never needs to say anything about children. Cory chose this over an explicit exception.
- "On site" means physical proximity, not role; everyone in the room affirms the universal safety terms (D02).

**Visitor and host.** (§3.7)

- **Whoever travels to someone else's space may always bring a chaperone** — a free pass, no permission needed, cannot be declined.
- **Whoever owns the space may not add observers.** The visitor is the exposed party; the host already holds every advantage.
- **Household members** (spouse, parent) are **disclosed like anyone else and sign off on their presence, or agree to stay out of the room.**

**The "witness" is the emergency contact.** (B4) **SUPERSEDES** the 2026-10-05 framing of an optional "witness signature" as an evidence feature.

- It is the ordinary emergency-contact field, and that is right precisely because it is banal: nobody reads it as an accusation.
- **What it attests to:** simply that **someone she trusts knows this shoot is happening.** Its presence tells everyone in the room that a specific person outside the shoot knows exactly who she is with.
- It is **not** a proxy, **not** a witness of identity, **not** a witness of presence, and **not** a witness of the signing act. It creates no authority.
- (The old brief builder already had an emergency-contact-on-set field. It was never understood as doing this job.)
