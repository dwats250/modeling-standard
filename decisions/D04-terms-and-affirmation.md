## Cory's answer

Partial direct ruling — 2026-10-05.

**Every term is individually affirmable and is the unit of the record.**

For speed, ordinary terms may offer a convenience control that checks a tightly labelled group, but the underlying terms remain individually represented.

**Body and money terms are never covered by that convenience control.** Anything about a person's body or money requires its own deliberate individual affirmation. *(The body/money framing is superseded 2026-10-15: there is no taxonomy; a product-owned fixed list of named terms stays outside the convenience. See below.)*

This modifies the July statement that every field must always be clicked separately: individual term evidence remains, while ordinary terms may be batch-checked as an interaction convenience.

### Direct rulings — 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-14/15

Source: [`2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md`](2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md). Later than the ruling above; governs where they conflict. Scope: photo shoots only.

**Requiredness.** (Q2, Q6, A3)

- **The product decides what is required.** A drafter cannot promote their own preferences into required terms. "The app exists to protect. It does not exist to solidify somebody's vision." Bring-red-heels is professional communication, not a term.
- **Required means anything protecting physical or emotional safety.** Compensation and deliverables are inside that scope, because breaking the deal harms people.
- **There are no accidental blanks in anything that reaches another person.** A field left blank at creation must be explicitly marked **decide-together**; otherwise the project cannot be made shareable. Every field is complete before presenting, in one state or the other.

**Fixed deliberate-tick list, and the group-check convenience.** (Q6, §4)

- The question "who decides a term is about body or money" was **rejected as mis-framed. There is no taxonomy.** Money is not a category; it is one field, the fee.
- The rule is **a short fixed list of named terms that always need their own deliberate tick**, set by the product. Cory named: **the fee, the coverage level, physical contact, the stop rule.** No further members are added here.
- **Every term remains individually represented and individually recorded.** Ordinary terms may use a bulk/group-check convenience; the fixed deliberate-tick terms are outside it.
- **Sequence preserved:** the 2026-10-05 ruling allowed group-check with "body and money" excluded; a 2026-10-09 statement said every individual field is acted on individually, not by sections; the 2026-10-14/15 ruling settles it as above (**SUPERSEDES** the 2026-10-09 statement and the body/money framing). Whether someone used group-check is never shown to others (Q16; noise doctrine).

**Who controls a term.** (Q7, §3.11) **SUPERSEDES** the reading of terms as symmetric proposals.

- **The subject of a term controls it.** If a term is about a person, that person controls the value or boundary.
- A counterparty cannot override or negotiate that person's declaration. The counterparty's choice is to **accept the resulting terms or walk away** from the collaboration. ("If she declares 'don't touch my hair,' the other party cannot decline it.")
- Terms are therefore not symmetrical: some are **declarations** by the person they concern; others are **requests** about someone else, which that person may decline.
- **Open parameters — a third shape: "you set this one."** A creator may hand a term to its subject to declare their own value or ceiling, rather than accept someone else's.
- **Safety terms cannot be dropped or left out**, including from a later version. (Q7, A5b)

**Route-specific acceptance.** (Q15; recorded in D02.) Posted work: applying is wholesale acceptance; direct send: per-term. What is written and accepted is closed; what is absent remains to be settled. For affirmation evidence this means the two routes reach "all green" by different acts; how that is evidenced is a later technical question.

**Ticking versus signing.** (Q12) **SUPERSEDES** any reading of per-term affirmation as signing.

- **Ticking a term and signing are two different acts.**
- Everyone ticks terms until every term is green for every person. **Only then does anyone sign.** Nobody signs a document that can still change.
- **Implication for S05 (understanding only; no code change here):** the S05 rows titled "per-term affirmation evidence" are tick evidence. They are not the signature and do not make anything final. See `docs/engineering/OCTOBER-SEMANTIC-IMPACT.md`.

**Coverage ceiling.** (B6)

- **An agreed coverage ceiling is a hard cap.** Nothing agreed informally on the day expands it; on-the-day agreement is not recorded at all.
- **Exceeding it voids the entire agreement**, not just the extra frames. Modeling Standard does not itself void anything: the force comes from a **signed contract term** stating that exceeding the agreed terms voids the agreement. No legal wording is designed here, and "void" is not interpreted beyond Cory's product requirement.
- **Purpose:** to remove later hearsay about alleged on-the-day permission. It **protects all parties**, including a photographer against someone offering to exceed in order to create a claim.

**Evidence kept.** (Q17) **A decided product requirement**, not a security recommendation: document fingerprint, IP, device, chained timestamps, and a retained audit and version trail, kept as long as the document. Held in the record, never shown to participants (recording versus showing, doctrine 2). The chain is not designed here.

**No means no.** (Q13)

- **A safety term every person affirms individually.**
- The tagline — "No is supposed to be an answer to a question, not a response to an action" — goes on the page and into the core values, **and** the signed acknowledgment stays on every brief. Both, not one.
- **Same term, two faces.** For the person protected it reads as the tools she has (safe word, guarantee, stop anything at any moment). For the people bound by it, it is the acknowledgment of the obligation. Audience-specific presentation of one underlying term.
- **Exact wording is Cory's to write and remains OPEN.**

Cross-cutting doctrine that applies here: noise doctrine; recording versus showing; new standard, not the existing one (industry omissions are no authority); over-protection is itself a harm.

**Still open in this file (photo-shoot scope):**

1. **No-means-no wording** — Cory's to write.
2. **The visible signing act** — what signing looks like once all is green (typed name, drawn signature or other) is not answered by these rulings.
3. **Reconciliation question R1** — "a no is not recorded" (Q10) against "every version is retained" (Q17): whether a dropped term persists in the evidentiary version history. Needed before the evidence record for resends is designed.
4. Representation of trade or non-money value beyond the single fee field was not addressed. It is not needed for the D01 scenario (no money changed hands) and is not queued as homework.

The rule for what is required is decided (above). The concrete required-term list for the D01 shoot has not been drafted; it is to be drafted against that rule and checked by Cory, like the document trigger table in D08.
