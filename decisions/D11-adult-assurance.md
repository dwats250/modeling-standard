# D11 — Adult assurance

Status: OPEN — only whether a synthetic demonstration with invented people needs verification remains; timing is decided

## Why this needs Cory

The assurance method for real participation is now decided. What remains is timing for synthetic demonstration and whether the anti-bot / anti-ban-evasion purpose moves verification earlier in the journey.

For information: the first stages are adult-only and contain no youth path, no guardian path, and no placeholder for either. That partition is designed when Cory and counsel open it.

## Cory's answer

Partial direct ruling — 2026-10-05.

Real verification is required; self-attestation is not an acceptable substitute for real participation.

Verification also serves an anti-bot and anti-ban-evasion purpose: it is an identity-uniqueness control as well as adult assurance.

The July rule to retain only the verification result and never the identity document was not re-confirmed in this session and remains candidate evidence, not a newly ratified detail.

Still open at that date: whether a synthetic demonstration with invented people requires verification; whether verification must move earlier than first participation because of the anti-bot or ban-evasion role; and later vendor or mechanism choices. *(Timing answered 2026-10-15; see below.)*

### Direct rulings — 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-14/15

Source: [`2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md`](2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md) (Q1, C4, §3.9, §3.5).

- **Verification stays at first participation.** (Q1) Sign-in (D03) is the door, not identity.
- **Bots are not the justification.** Cory dismissed them: "nothing to farm here, Instagram is infinitely more lucrative."
- **Ban evasion alone justifies verification** — it is what makes a removal stick. This **refines** the 2026-10-05 statement that verification serves anti-bot and anti-ban-evasion purposes: the anti-bot purpose is not relied on.
- **Identity verification is one of the justified specialist third parties.** Third parties are used only when a specialist means Modeling Standard holds less; a verification provider is precisely what lets the platform **hold only a boolean and a date**, per "none of it belongs to us." (§3.9) This is Cory's direction on retention from the provider. No provider is chosen here.
- **The product is adult** (C4; see D12). The deferred youth path stays deferred, with zero UI hints.

Direction: the product's existence filters bad actors at the door, so people inside are not treated as presumptive suspects (§3.5). This is not a security guarantee.

**Still open:** whether a synthetic demonstration with invented people requires verification. These rulings do not address it, and it is not inferred. It does not block the first photo-shoot product semantics, and the current demo has no participant flow. Vendor and mechanism choices are Dustin's, within the boolean-and-date direction.
