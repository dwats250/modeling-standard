### Direct rulings — 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-14/15

Source: [`2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md`](2026-10-09-15-cory-reconciliation.md) (A2, Q14, Q16, Q10, §3). Scope: photo shoots only.

**Layers.** Three, not two: universal safety; **shared logistics** (the call sheet — schedule, call times, location, seen by everyone involved including each other's slots); **private money**. **Money is the only private thing.** Craft terms, deliverables and usage are shared. **SUPERSEDES** the 2026-10-05 statement that commercial and craft terms are the private layer. "Two people work for the same company, other people can't just look up their contracts."

**The grid, answered.**

| Information | Ruling |
|---|---|
| **Counterparties** | **Mutually visible from the start** inside a collaboration. "This is a modeling and photography business, its sole purpose is visual." Discovery stays one-directional; identity inside a collaboration is mutual. |
| **Contact details** | **Personal phone and email only once both sides commit to the job.** Socials and professional presence are visible. (The attack Cory named: post a job, collect fifty applicants' details, cancel, cold-call.) |
| **Exact location** | **Exposure follows initiative.** Whoever posts puts themselves out there, so their information is available; the protection is against fishing for everyone else's. A **general area** (nearest intersection or station) may be shown first, with the full address later; exact address up front is also fine. **Location type is settled before contact; the exact address is a required term locked before signing.** |
| **Who has affirmed what** | **A live shared document.** Everyone watches it fill in green. Not back-and-forth. |
| **Roster** | **Visible to everyone before agreeing**, because everyone green-lights every person present. |
| **Compensation** | **Private. The only private thing.** |
| **Usage terms** | Shared (they are craft and deliverable terms, no longer private). |
| **Private notes** | **Do not exist.** No DM channel; all dialogue is term-bound. |
| **Concerns raised / a "no"** | **Not recorded** (Q10). The final whitelist is the record: if a term is absent from the final contract, it is not agreed. This is not "hidden from participants"; it is not kept. Its interaction with retained versions is reconciliation question R1 (D04). |
| **Change history** | **Held in the evidentiary record, never shown.** |
| **The final record** | **Everything jointly agreed, plus the person's own payment terms, and nobody else's numbers.** What each person walks away holding is the contract (D05). This decides what each person's record contains; whether it is stored as one record with per-party views or as separate artifacts is a technical choice that must deliver exactly that content. |

The 2026-10-05 line "a client may see commercial numbers where entitled" stays consistent with this: a party sees their own payment terms, never anyone else's.

**What the organizer sees that others do not.** No organizer-only view was ruled. Exposure follows initiative (location), and there is no organizer confirmation step (D02). Nothing in these rulings gives the creator extra visibility.

**On site.** Physical proximity, not role (D02). Everyone in the room sees and affirms the universal safety terms.

**Noise doctrine.** (Q16, §3.1) People see **what** was agreed, never **how** someone went about agreeing. Not exposed to participants: response times, read receipts, revision counts, edit history, opened-and-didn't-reply, whether someone used a group-check. This is about what data exists for participants at all, not tone. **Recording versus showing** (§3.2): the evidentiary record keeps the invisible metadata Cory requires (D04); participants are shown only what was agreed.

Also governing: over-protection is itself a harm (no manufactured fear); the app's existence does half the protection — users inside are not presumptive suspects (direction, not a security guarantee).

**Status for the photo-shoot scope.** Every row of the grid, the organizer question and the final-record question are answered. Fashion-show visibility (where a model has no relationship with the other models) is deliberately not covered.
