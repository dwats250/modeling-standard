## Decisions reached

### D01 — first scenario

Two adults who already know each other. The model created the project and sent it directly to a photographer she knew. No money. Coverage, touch and wardrobe-adjustment expectations were discussed. The photographer shot, edited and delivered files. A possible future album-cover use by the model was discussed; the photographer's own usage was not.

Posting versus direct sending is a distribution choice, not a different product.

Creator is role-neutral: creator does not imply photographer, payer, creative author or obligation holder.

### D02 — topology, finality and response

Commercial and craft agreements are private and pairwise under the project. Safety and consent terms are universal for everyone on site.

An agreement is final only when every party named in that agreement has affirmed every required term.

No counters. A non-accepted term may be dropped and resent, or the proposal may be withdrawn.

Cancellation, roster changes, withdrawal-before-finality and amendment behaviour remain open.

### D04 — affirmation

Each term remains an individually represented unit of evidence.

Ordinary terms may offer a group-check convenience. Body and money terms always require their own deliberate individual action.

### D07 — paywall

The free tier completes the full loop: project/brief, agreement and record.

Monetization is by capacity, not by preventing completion. Approximately two active collaborations was an example, not a fixed entitlement.

This supersedes the older documents-only free-tier candidate direction.

### D09 — removal

Ordinary in-product removal of a shared record requires a reason and approval by all parties.

Statutory erasure remains a separate legal lane and requires counsel.

### D11 — verification

Real verification is required for real participation; attestation is not a substitute.

Verification also serves anti-bot and anti-ban-evasion identity-uniqueness purposes. Exact timing remains open.

### D12 — authority

No proxies. Nobody signs for another person. The person acting must be the person reading and affirming.

The current build is adult-only. Witnessing, where used, is separate from authority.

### D08 — documents

A photo release is confirmed as an essential document for the first scenario because contemplated album-cover usage is commercial.

### D10 — visibility

One model does not see another model's commercial agreement. A client can see commercial numbers where entitled. Safety and consent terms are common to everyone on site; private commercial and craft terms remain between the parties they concern.

## Additional direct product rulings

These rulings are preserved here without forcing each into a new decision number.

1. Applying is acceptance of published terms. The product should remove bargaining after selection; terms and value are visible before someone raises their hand.
2. One number, one answer. Money and terms are not counter-offered. A term may be removed; it is not haggled.
3. Unfilled fields are bounded dialogue. Conversation may attach to the specific term being completed, including text or voice memo; the completed brief is the output.
4. Field ordering replaces hard brief types. Safety and non-negotiables first, variable creative fields later. Templates are starting structures, not different product machines.
5. Three field buckets: settled before contact opens; required before shooting but completable through dialogue; optional. Deliberately open and accidentally unfinished are different states.
6. Escalation requires a neutral record and a mandatory delay before acceptance. No explanation is demanded from the person whose boundary is changing.
7. Recorded term-bound dialogue is not a private deniable DM channel.
8. Visibility is one-directional by default. Work can be posted; talent is not browsable. Talent deliberately grants temporary access to personal or professional material.
9. Three known starts: talent applies to posted work; someone directly sends a project to a known person; talent converts an off-platform approach into a Modeling Standard brief. Whether a fourth exists is open.
10. Witness signature may be optional.
11. Universal safety terms; pairwise commercial terms.
12. Payment has no natural direction. Any named party may owe value to any other named party. Creator is not payer by implication.
13. Who asked first must not determine who pays. Value must be explicit rather than inferred from initiation direction.
14. No means no. "No is supposed to be an answer to a question, not a response to an action." Cory wants the value stated publicly and a signed acknowledgment on every brief.
15. Role neutrality. Creator, photographer, payer, creative author and obligation holder are independent concepts.
